# AIPIL (COSMOPOLITAN) – YIN | IDEAS

The IDEAS programme is delivered at Cosmopolitan University, Abuja.

## Two actions are offered

- **Apply for Admission**
- **My Admission Status** — "Check your admission status and take the first step
  toward a brighter future with cutting-edge technology training!"

## What the programme is described as

A technology training programme offering "cutting-edge technology training".

## Skill tracks offered

The programme runs five skill tracks:

1. AI
2. Software Development
3. Creative & Digital Marketing
4. Enterprise Digital Skills
5. Drone / Emerging Technology

Participants complete a capstone project as the final requirement, submitted
either individually or as an approved team.
