# Contact the Centre for Climate-Smart Agriculture

The Centre for Climate-Smart Agriculture (CCSA) has its own office, separate from
the university's main administration, and its own contact address. Enquiries about
the centre, its programmes, or its work go to the centre, not to the main switchboard.

Centre for Climate-Smart Agriculture (CCSA)
Wuse II, Abuja, Nigeria
Email: ccsa@cosmopolitan.edu.ng

The centre sits within the university's new Faculty of Climate-Smart Agriculture and
Sustainability, which is developing the programmes listed on the centre's own site.
Questions about admission to those programmes should be directed to the centre first.
