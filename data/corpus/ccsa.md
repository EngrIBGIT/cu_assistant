# Centre for Climate-Smart Agriculture (CCSA)

The Centre for Climate-Smart Agriculture and IFPRI are partnering with
Cosmopolitan University to establish a **Faculty of Climate-Smart Agriculture and
Sustainability** in Abuja. NUC teams have visited the university to verify
resources as part of the process, including for an MBA programme focused on
agribusiness and renewable energy management.

## Programmes under development

Three academic programmes were identified for the new faculty:

- **Agronomy and Crop Production**
- **Agricultural Extension and Rural Innovation** — with a focus on digital
  extension services
- **Agribusiness**

## Research and Innovation

Develop, test, and promote climate-smart agricultural technologies and practices
to improve productivity and resource use, build climate resilience, and support
smallholder farmers.

## Capacity Building

Train and equip farmers, cooperatives, and extension agents, with emphasis on
rural youth and women in agriculture.

## Digital Transformation

Deploy cloud computing, mobile technologies, AI and data analytics, the Internet
of Things, satellite imagery, advanced manufacturing, drones, and robotics to
deliver practical solutions for smallholder farmers, including real-time
information, precision-farming tools, early warnings, and improved access to
markets.

## AI-powered advisory work

A documented initiative uses AI and digital technologies to enhance
climate-smart agricultural practices and improve productivity, resilience, and
sustainability for Nigerian farmers, supported by the Farmers Information
Management System (FIMS) portal.
