# Cosmopolitan Film School

"Join Cosmopolitan Film School and dive into the world of cinema, television, and
streaming. Our comprehensive diploma equips you with the skills to excel in the
entertainment industry."

## Purpose

"Our mission is to provide comprehensive training in film studies that covers
eight crucial aspects of filmmaking and prepares students for careers in
directing, producing, screenwriting, and editing."

## Programme

A complete diploma in film studies covering eight career-focused areas, with
study of cinematography, video editing, screenwriting, and sound design.

## Institution

Cosmopolitan University Film School, Cosmopolitan University, Abuja, Nigeria.
