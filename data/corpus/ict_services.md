# IT Service Department

"Providing innovative technology solutions and support to empower the university
community in teaching, learning, and research."

## Our Units

- **Software Development Unit** — development and maintenance of university
  software systems, portals, and applications.
- **Networking Unit** — management of campus network infrastructure,
  connectivity, and security.
- **Support Unit** — frontline technical support and administrative assistance
  for staff and students.
- **Interns** — interns and graduates gaining hands-on experience in the
  department's units.

## Quick Access Services

The department lists the following quick-access services. Note that only the
Student Portal has a separately published web address in the index; the others
