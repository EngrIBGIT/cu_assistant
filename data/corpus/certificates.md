## Artificial-intelligence-related certificates

Six of the fourteen are explicitly AI programmes: CUCAI (ICT and Artificial
Intelligence), CUCIC (AI for Intelligence and Crime Management), CUCIF
(Artificial Intelligence for Finance), CUCAIB (Artificial Intelligence for
Businesses), CUCAIL (Artificial Intelligence for Law Practice), and CUCAR
(Artificial Intelligence Applications in Robotics).
